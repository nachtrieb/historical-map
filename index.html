Empires by year: Chronas borders, HYDE 3.5 population

Source: Chronas (api.chronas.org) rulers by year and province polygons; population from HYDE 3.5, gridded and intersected with the polygons (chronas_hyde.py). Borders are province-sized, so values are approximate.

Data

  • Borders and rulers: Chronas (chronas.org; data from api.chronas.org, pulled 2026-10-15). Provinces are coloured whole, so an empire's area is the territory Chronas claims for it, not effective control.
  • Population: HYDE 3.5 baseline (Klein Goldewijk, Beusen, Vander Linden, Doelman; Utrecht University, 2025; doi:10.24416/UU01-F45D44, CC BY), intersected with the Chronas provinces.

Using this page

  • The slider and the −10, −1, +1, +10 buttons change the year. With a keyboard: arrow keys, Shift or Ctrl for ten years, Home and End.
  • Touch or click a chart to set the year; drag along it to scrub.
  • The strip under the slider sets the years the charts show: drag the middle to slide it, the edges to zoom. “Around year” shows 100 years; “All years” resets.
  • The Empires button chooses which curves are drawn.
